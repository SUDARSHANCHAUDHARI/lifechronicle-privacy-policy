# LifeChronicle Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** March 21, 2026
_Last updated: March 21, 2026_
**Version:** 1.0.0

LifeChronicle ("we," "our," or "us") is a life journaling app for Android. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles your data.

## Information We Collect

### Data Stored Locally
- Journal entries (text, photos, optional location tags) are stored exclusively on your device using Room database.
- No account or sign-in is required.

### Location Data (Optional)
- If you choose to tag an entry with your location, that location is stored locally within the entry.
- Location is never transmitted to any server automatically.

## How We Use Your Information

- Store and display your journal entries, photos, and location tags within the app.
- Generate PDF exports of your chronicle on request (exported locally to your device).

We do not transmit your journal data to any SudarshanTechLabs server. We do not sell or share your data.

## Storage and Retention

- All data is stored exclusively on your device.
- Photos are stored in app-private storage.
- Data is retained until you delete individual entries or uninstall the app.

## Data Security

- Local data is protected by Android's built-in storage encryption.

## Permissions Used

- **READ_MEDIA_IMAGES / CAMERA**: Required to attach photos to journal entries.
- **ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION / ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION**: Used optionally to tag entries with your location when you request it.
- **WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE**: Required to export your chronicle as a PDF to your device.

## Your Rights and Controls

- Delete any entry or photo at any time within the app.
- Export your full chronicle as a PDF at any time.
- Revoke location permission in Android Settings → Apps → LifeChronicle → Permissions.

## Children's Privacy

LifeChronicle is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy to reflect new features or legal requirements. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance.
